Buckets

    DeFi total value lockedtracked, not scored

    Capital deposited in DeFi protocols, from DefiLlama. Deliberately outside the model: it is denominated in USD, so it rises when token prices rise, and only a small share sits on Bitcoin.

    Daily TVL and BTC returns correlate at only 0.18, because the pegged-stablecoin share of deposits has no price component at all. Useful as corroboration that on-chain activity has not rolled over, not as a regime input.

    ETF flows, daily

    Net creations less redemptions across every US spot fund, by session. Hover or tab through the chart to read a day; the panels share an x-axis, so the crosshair moves through both.

    Each panel has its own vertical scale — bitcoin flows run several times the size of ether’s, so bar heights are not comparable between the two panels, only within one. Green is money in, red is money out; the position above or below the zero line carries that too, so the chart does not rely on colour alone. Sessions are ETF trading days, and flows publish with a one-day lag. The four-week sums in the readout are the figures D1 and D3 actually score.
